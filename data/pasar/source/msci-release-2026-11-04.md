[Skip to content](https://ir.msci.com/news-releases/news-release-details/msci-november-index-review-announcement-scheduled-november-11#page-f141b693c3) # Investor News ## MSCI November Index Review Announcement Scheduled for November 11, 2026 November 4, 2026 [PDF Version](https://ir.msci.com/node/23016/pdf) LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Nov. 4, 2026-- MSCI Inc. (NYSE:MSCI), a leading provider of critical decision support tools and services for the global investment community, will announce the results of the November 2026 Index Review for the MSCI Equity Indexes - including the MSCI Global Standard, MSCI Global Small Cap and MSCI Micro Cap Indexes, the MSCI Global Value and Growth Indexes, the MSCI Frontier Markets, and MSCI Frontier Markets Small Cap Indexes, the MSCI US Equity Indexes, the MSCI US REIT Index, the MSCI China A Onshore indexes and the MSCI China All Shares Indexes. All changes will be made as of the close of November 30, 2026. MSCI will post the list of additions to and deletions from the indexes for the November 2026 Index Review on its web site, [www.msci.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.msci.com&esheet=54635648&newsitemid=20261104003393&lan=en-US&anchor=www.msci.com&index=1&md5=c3b30f20ef5f78b6c0dcb3ae284cb376), shortly after 11:00 p.m. Central European Time (CET) on November 11, 2026. A summary of the announcement will be made available shortly thereafter on Bloomberg page MSCN, and Reuters public page MSCIA. Additionally, MSCI will make detailed rebalancing information available to clients beginning immediately after the summary announcement appears on Bloomberg and/or Reuters. Clients can access the subscriber section of each index at: [www.msci.com/index-review-subscribers](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.msci.com%2Findex-review-subscribers&esheet=54635648&newsitemid=20261104003393&lan=en-US&anchor=www.msci.com%2Findex-review-subscribers&index=2&md5=b1c268973cb1648676a587b41d920242) For the MSCI US Equity Indexes and the MSCI US REIT Index, a summary of the announcement will be made available at [www.msci.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.msci.com&esheet=54635648&newsitemid=20261104003393&lan=en-US&anchor=www.msci.com&index=3&md5=92773d1d2fa82c825cd847f4a4fb704a).
